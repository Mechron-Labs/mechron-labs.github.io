Our thesis: automated physical work will be bought as a service, at scale. Companies don't want machines; they want the work done.

Physical work by subscription.

Robots for repetitive tasks indoors: on a mobile base when the task involves moving around, or fixed on a bench when the material comes to the robot. We start with light industry and distribution centers. The customer pays a monthly fee for the work delivered.

Installation

Hardware assembled in Brazil from imported components. Goes into operation with no integration project.

Training

Learns a new task in a few hours, by demonstration. Someone on the floor shows it, and the robot takes over.

Operations

Remote fleet operations and maintenance, included in the monthly fee.

The robot learns a task in hours, by demonstration.

Demonstration

We perform the task by teleoperation a few times. The robot records what it sees and what it does.

Imitation learning

The model learns the motion from the recordings. In operation, it receives the instruction, looks at the scene and executes.

Fleet

All robots share the same model. Operating data from each unit feeds back into training, and the whole fleet improves with every release.

In traditional robotics every task is programmed by hand, which is why every installation becomes a months-long project. Here the same robot switches tasks with a new instruction, with no reprogramming. That is what makes the monthly fee possible, and why the platform gets better as the fleet grows.
